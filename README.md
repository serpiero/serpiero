### 👋 Hey, I'm Ser Piero

I love music and coding. I aim to be the best producer I can be and create amazing audio plugins and Max for Live devices along the way.

I also build tools for my everyday use that you might find useful. Most of my ideas start while making music, organizing my files, or customizing my workspace.

🎛️ **Music comes first.** Coding lets me build the tools I wish I had.  
🐧 **Linux is my playground.** I like making my setup look and work the way I want.  
💡 **Always learning.** There's usually another idea waiting to become a project.
